

# Threadz

**Facebook watch out — this is better and you can text your selfies.**

Open source, federated, self-hostable communication. Built on email.
Works on 2G. Owned by the people who use it.
No ads. No tracking. No feed. No back doors. No permission required.

MIT licensed, forever.

---

## What it is

Threadz is a social communication system that any sovereign can run
without asking anyone. A nation, a city, a university, a co-op, a
community, a family, one person — anyone can host their own instance,
and instances talk to each other over the open internet, the way email
already does.

You get:
- Your own address
- A view of your people as shapes, not a feed
- Photos, messages, and threads that work on a slow connection
- Data that stays on your instance, under your rules
- Federation with other Threadz instances, and with normal email

You don't get:
- Ads
- Tracking
- An algorithm choosing what you see
- A company between you and your people

---

## Why

Because the last open messaging system — email — was federated, boring,
and worked for forty years. Then the walled gardens won. Now your
identity, your photos, and your relationships live on someone else's
servers, under someone else's rules, shaped by someone else's business
model.

Threadz is the version where you own the thing.

Same instinct as every open tool that lasts: the user should own it,
and nobody should need permission to talk to their own people.

---

## What it isn't

- Not a company.
- Not a product with a roadmap and a growth team.
- Not a competitor to be acquired.
- Not a place with ads, tracking, engagement metrics, or a feed.
- Not finished. Not perfect. V1.
